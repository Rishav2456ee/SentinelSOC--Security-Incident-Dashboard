How the risk score works

Every incident earns points by severity: Critical = 10, High = 6, Medium = 3, Low = 1. All points from one source IP are added up. The IP with the most points gets 100, and every other IP is scaled against it:

risk score = (IP points ÷ highest IP points) × 100

Tiers: 80+ Critical, 60+ High, 40+ Medium, below 40 Low. The score is relative to the loaded data. Click any IP to open its incidents.
